Workshop overview

A stronger process. A better customer experience.

This four-hour session brings together the first three quarters of the 2026 leadership series taught in DFW and Austin. Houston leaders will connect the full customer journey to their daily work, identify where handoffs and service can break down, and develop specific improvements to test before November’s annual managers’ training in Marble Falls.

The idea behind the series
“The purpose of a beehive is not to make honey. Honey is the byproduct of a healthy hive.”
Seth Godin · The Song of Significance

For ABC, a healthy hive means people who are supported, processes that work, and handoffs that set the next person up for success. The honey is what that creates for customers: problems solved, homes and businesses cared for, and confidence that ABC will follow through.

The shared framework for Houston, Austin, and DFW

Customer success is the goal

Customer Journey + Customer Experience + Customer Service = Customer Success. The framework connects how we organize the work to how the customer feels, what we deliver, and the result the customer receives.

The path

Customer Journey

The full path from the first call to a long-term relationship. Each step, owner, and handoff should help the customer move forward with clarity.

+
The feeling

Customer Experience

How that path feels to the customer: the effort required, the trust earned, and the confidence they have at each step.

+
The work

Customer Service

What we actually deliver: the technical work, human care, communication, and follow-through in every interaction, visit, and invoice.

=
The result

Customer Success

The customer’s need is met, the promise is kept, and the customer feels cared for. That is the foundation for lasting trust, loyalty, and recommendations.

Process mapping makes the journey visible. The workshop then connects that map to the experience, service, and customer outcomes it should produce.

How the learning becomes action

One process, improved throughout the morning

Teaching, video, table discussion, and hands-on exercises work together. Teams carry the same process map through all three chapters, building a practical improvement they can put to work.

Map the work

Make the handoffs visible

Identify the steps, owners, and information each person needs. Locate a breakdown that is happening now and a risk the team can address before it happens.

Improve the experience

Reduce customer effort

Find where customers wait, repeat themselves, or lack clarity. Define a specific service behavior and how the team will know it happened.

Own the outcome

Prevent, recover, follow through

Clarify how the team prevents a miss, responds when something goes wrong, and confirms the resolution. Assign a first action, an owner, and a date.
